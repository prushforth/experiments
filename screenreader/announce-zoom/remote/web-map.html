Announce zoom, remote MapML in a <map is="web-map">

A <map> with one URL in it, a search box it gets from that URL's document, and a live region the viewer has to build somewhere other than its own shadow root. Three things that could each quietly not happen; if searching still moves the map and the move is still announced, none of them did.

MapML.js (Leaflet)

Two shadow roots: the one the <map> builds for its chrome and live region, and the one the layer keeps its fetched document in. The search control has to look into the second from the first.

MapML-MapLibre

In WebKit, where customized built-ins are not implemented, this frame stays an inert <map>: nothing to search, and nothing to announce.

MapML-OpenLayers

The announcement handler asks the viewer for its render root rather than assuming a shadow root, which is why the <output> ends up somewhere a screen reader can still find it.
