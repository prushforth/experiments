Restaurant locations near Dows Lake

Two things a page asks a map to do after it has loaded: go somewhere the reader named, and show what is around it. A place search over the NRCan GeoNames service turns a result into <map-feature> elements with M.geojson2mapml() and asks the new layer to zoomTo() itself; a button then clones a restaurant <map-layer> into the map, whose <map-link rel="features"> fetches whatever falls in the view. Both layers have to become named, focusable content the instant they are appended — which is the part that is easy to get wrong and impossible to see in a screenshot.

The content type is the restaurant layer's: inline, the <template> holds the whole layer, extent, cuisine <map-select> and feature template; remote, it holds a single <map-layer src="restaurants.mapml"> and the rest is fetched when the reader asks for it. The place layer is built from GeoJSON at runtime in every cell — a third kind of content that is neither written in the page nor fetched as MapML.

Every cell is driven by the same search.js, which finds its map with mapml-viewer, map[is="web-map"] and then uses nothing but the DOM.
