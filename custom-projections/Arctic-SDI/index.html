Lambert Equal Area Azimuthal

Seven Arctic nations each publish their own view of the Arctic, and each one centres the map on its own part of it — so the seven documents are in six different projections. Each one offers the other six as alternatives in the layer's settings, and choosing one turns the whole map to face that country.

MapML-MapLibre

MapLibre fetches its tiles rather than loading them as images, so it can only draw tiles from a server that allows cross-origin requests, and the Arctic SDI's does not: the map stays blank. The projections themselves work — choosing another country in the layer's settings still turns the map to face it — but the outlines of the other countries stop at the edge of the projection's bounds, where the other two draw them past it.
