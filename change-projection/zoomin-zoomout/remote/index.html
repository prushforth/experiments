Experiments

Change projection on zoom change, from remote MapML

Four maps of the same part of the world, drawn four different ways: a view from over the pole while you are far out, a map of Canada as you come closer, and a Web Mercator map once you are close in. Zoom and the map turns to the way of drawing that suits where you are, and the layer that is good for it appears. Each layer says what it is good for in its own media attribute, so the page only has to pick the projection. Here each layer is a separate MapML document, fetched and parsed as XML.

MapML.js (Leaflet)

The reference implementation. Zoom all the way out and in again to pass through all four maps — but crossing between the Atlas of Canada map and the Canada Base Map, at zoom 5 to 6, it never settles. Changing the projection also moves the zoom by one, which puts the map back in the range of the map it just left, so the two keep swapping and nothing is drawn.

MapML-MapLibre

MapML-MapLibre draws Web Mercator only.

MapML-OpenLayers

The same four documents and the same four media attributes, drawn by OpenLayers. Here the zoom survives the projection change, so every boundary settles.

What to try: zoom out one step at a time from the opening view and watch the layer list — the layer that does not suit the new projection and zoom stops being drawn, and the one that does takes over.
